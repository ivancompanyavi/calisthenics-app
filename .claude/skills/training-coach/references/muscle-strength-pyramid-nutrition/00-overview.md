# The Muscle & Strength Pyramid: Nutrition (Helms/Valdez/Morgan, 3e) — book overview

The evidence-based nutrition companion to the Training volume. Recommendations synthesize the
nutrition-science literature — a primary `[SCIENCE]`-tier source. 297 pp., organized as a priority
**pyramid**: lower levels matter more, set them first.

> **⚠️ Coaching guardrail (from SKILL.md / `docs/coaching-standards.md`):** this is *reference
> knowledge*, not a license to prescribe. The coach gives **trend-based diet guidance only** —
> bodyweight direction vs. goal — and does **not** invent calorie/macro targets for the athlete.
> Use this book to explain *why* a direction is right and what the evidence says, not to hand out a
> meal plan. (The deferred Phase-3 nutrition-standards pass still needs Ivan's explicit go.)

## The pyramid (file per unit)
- **Intro — mindset & tracking** (`00-intro-tracking`) — to track or not; how to track; **bodyweight-tracking method** (weigh-in + averaging + normal 1–2% daily noise).
- **Level 1 — Energy Balance** (`01`) — THE CORE for a cut/bulk: maintenance estimation, cut-vs-bulk decision, **rates of loss (0.5–1%/wk, tiered by body-fat)** and gain, metabolic adaptation, energy availability/REDs, cardio for fat loss, body recomposition.
- **Level 2 — Macronutrients** (`02`) — protein g/kg (higher in a deficit), carb/fat split, worked calculations.
- **Level 3 — Micronutrients** (`03`) — healthy-eating guidelines, fiber, fruit/veg, fluid, sodium.
- **Level 4 — Nutrient Timing** (`04`) — diet breaks & refeeds (protocols), meal frequency/TRF, protein distribution, peri-workout.
- **Level 5 — Supplementation** (`05`) — the tier system: which supplements have evidence (Tier 1/2), doses, safety/quality.
- **Making Adjustments & Measuring Progress** (`06`) — **the trend-interpretation toolkit**: scale-weight assessment + the decision rules for when/how to adjust intake; visual/performance/circumference measures.
- **Peaking & Post-Diet Recovery** (`07`, condensed, competition-focused) — reverse dieting / recovery-diet mechanics (transferable to ending a cut); physique-peaking flagged competition-only.
- **Behavior & Lifestyle** (`08`) — motivation (SDT), goal pursuit, COM-B barriers, environment design — the adherence layer.

## Highest-leverage for a trend-based slow cut
`01` (rates, metabolic adaptation, energy availability) + `06` (how to read the weight trend and
adjust) + `02` (protein high during the deficit) + `08` (adherence). These map directly onto
`coach-report.mjs`'s bodyweight-delta signal and the strength-to-weight goal.

# References — Knowledge Base Index (router)

Distilled, page-cited notes from the coaching library. **Read this first**, then open the one
relevant chapter file — don't load the whole KB. Distilled files live under each book's folder;
raw extracted text is in `_sources/` (gitignored, grep-only); figures/charts live in the PDFs.

## Retrieval protocol
1. **This INDEX** → pick the chapter that owns the question.
2. **The distilled `.md`** → answers ~90% directly, with `(p.NN)` cites and `[SCIENCE]/[CONVENTION]/[DISPUTED]` tags.
3. **Exact wording / a claim you must quote verbatim** → `grep` `_sources/og2.txt`.
4. **Charts, figures, progression tables** → open the PDF (`docs/reference/…`) at the cited page (they're images, not in the text).

**Cite fidelity:** claims and section-level pages are verified faithful to the source. Exact page
numbers are reliable to ±1–2 (occasional drift near chapter boundaries); when precision matters, confirm via `_sources` grep.

## Overcoming Gravity 2e — chapter map
`overcoming-gravity/` · full book PDF: `docs/reference/Steven Low - Overcoming Gravity 2nd Edition PDF.pdf`
See `overcoming-gravity/` for one file per chapter. Highlights of what each owns:

- **Ch.1 principles** (pp.3–9) — SAID, progressive overload, leverage, the repetition continuum.
- **Ch.2 physiology** (pp.10–20) — strength defn, motor units/fiber types, 6 neural mechanisms, hypertrophy mechanisms, open/closed chain.
- **Ch.3 progression charts & goals** (pp.21–34) — the level system, goal-setting, exercise selection. *(Charts are PDF images pp.30–33.)*
- **Ch.4 structural balance** (pp.35–43) — shoulder health, push:pull ratios, the "why" of balance.
- **Ch.5 programming & hierarchy** (pp.44–58) — intra-workout progression, fitness-fatigue/supercompensation, periodization skeleton, attribute→frequency, **the 5-step session hierarchy**.
- **Ch.6 population** (pp.59–70) — tailoring by training history / age / sport / injury status.
- **Ch.7 constructing a routine** (pp.71–80) — full-body vs. split; the 4 splits (push/pull, upper/lower, straight/bent-arm, PPL).
- **Ch.8 warm-up & skill work** (pp.81–89) — warm-up structure (blood flow→mobility→positional), skill-work timing/type/dosage.
- **Ch.9 strength work** (pp.90–114) — **the numbers chapter**: concentric/isometric/eccentric notation, not-to-failure rule, Rule of 15, 1 rep ≈ 2s iso / ≈ 3s eccentric, isometrics at 60–75% max, eccentric chaining, sets, exercise order, rest, tempo, core.
- **Ch.10 methods of progression** (pp.115–136) — intra-exercise, inter-exercise, and complex/periodized progression (incl. DUP).
- **Ch.11 prehab/isolation/flexibility** (pp.137–150) — prehab & isolation placement, static vs. PNF vs. loaded stretching, mobility vs. flexibility.
- **Ch.12 mesocycle planning** (pp.151–170) — per-level exercises/scheduling/cycle-ending, deload, max testing, workout restructuring, elite programming.
- **Ch.13 endurance/cross-training/templates** (pp.171–187) — energy systems, GtG, ladders, MetCon, HIIT, cardio, cross- & hybrid templates.
- **Ch.14 overreaching/overtraining** (pp.188–194) — planned overreach as a tool, overtraining signs, RPE & logs.
- **Ch.15 health & injury management** (pp.195–211) — burn/pain/soreness, injury propensity, rehab goal-setting, connective-tissue protocols, RICE vs MEAT, working around injuries, picking a professional.
- **Ch.16 lifestyle** (pp.212–220) — sleep, nutrition basics, training while sick.
- **Ch.17 untrained beginner** (pp.221–230) · **Ch.18 trained beginner** (pp.231–238) · **Ch.19 intermediate** (pp.239–250) · **Ch.20 advanced** (pp.251–262) — full worked routines + progression per level.
- **Ch.21 common injuries** (pp.263–291) — tendonitis, strains, headaches, costochondritis, thoracic/scapula, low back, anterior instability, impingement, AC joint, radiculopathy, **wrist/forearm**, joint noises, cramps.

**Not distilled (lookup via PDF/grep):** Ch.22 prehab/mobility resources, Ch.23 exercise technique, Ch.24 handstands, Ch.25 pulling, Ch.26 pushing — these are movement-catalog reference, not coaching doctrine.

# Overcoming Gravity 2e — book overview

Steven Low's *Overcoming Gravity* (2nd ed., 598 pp.) is the reference text for bodyweight/gymnastics
strength and its programming. This folder distills the coaching-relevant chapters (1–21); the
movement catalogs (22–26) stay in the PDF.

## How the book is organized (5 parts)
1. **Fundamental knowledge base (Ch.1–6):** principles, physiology, progression charts, structural
   balance, programming theory + the session hierarchy, population considerations.
2. **Constructing your routine (Ch.7–12):** splits, warm-up/skill, the strength-work "numbers"
   chapter, progression methods, prehab/flexibility, mesocycle planning.
3. **Factors that influence training (Ch.13–16):** endurance/cross-training, over-reaching/-training,
   health & injury management, lifestyle.
4. **Program implementation (Ch.17–20):** full worked routines by level — untrained → advanced.
5. **Injury/prehab resources & exercises (Ch.21–26):** injury encyclopedia + movement catalogs.

## The load-bearing cross-cutting ideas
- **Session hierarchy (Ch.5):** warm-up → skill/technique → power/isometrics/eccentrics/strength →
  endurance → prehab/cool-down. Protect the highest-CNS work while fresh.
- **Intensity via progression difficulty, volume via reps→sets→exercises (Ch.5, Ch.9).**
- **Not-to-failure for strength; closer-to-failure for hypertrophy (Ch.9).**
- **Contraction equivalence (Ch.9):** 1 concentric rep ≈ 2 s isometric ≈ 3 s eccentric; isometrics
  programmed at 60–75% of max hold, not fixed seconds.
- **Weekly (not per-workout) volume accounting past the beginner stage (Ch.5, Ch.10).**
- **Mesocycles 4–8 wk ending in a deload; the plateau is the deload signal (Ch.5, Ch.12, Ch.14).**
- **Structural balance / push:pull (Ch.4)** and **prehab as programmed work (Ch.11)**.

## How this relates to `docs/coaching-standards.md`
OG2 is a `[CONVENTION]` source — one expert's system, internally consistent and well-reasoned, but
not meta-analytic consensus. Where the book cites physiology it's tagged `[SCIENCE]`; where coaches
disagree (e.g. rigid isometric hold gates) it's `[DISPUTED]`. The distilled files preserve those
tags so advice can distinguish "Low's method" from "established evidence." For hard evidence-tier
backing, pair with the science texts listed in `../BACKLOG.md` once acquired/distilled.
